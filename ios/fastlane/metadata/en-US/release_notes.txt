What’s New:
• A new app icon, made for the Liquid Glass look of iOS 26. It shows on your Live Activity too, and the alternate icons have a new picker in Settings → Appearance.
• Change the email on your account, and link or unlink Google and Apple sign-in, from the Account screen.
• Settings is redesigned. Instead of one long list, it opens on your account and six clear pages: Appearance, Language & region, Notifications, Brewing, Home screen, and Privacy & data.
• When you tap a grind size field, it now suggests the last three grind sizes you used.

Improvements:
• Sign-in is the same simple sheet everywhere in the app, and a verification code submits itself as soon as you’ve typed it.
• Choosing a brewing screen layout now shows a preview of each one, in Settings → Brewing and from the gear icon before a brew.
• Step change alerts (silent, vibration or sound) now have their own place in Settings → Brewing.
• Bean review reminders can now be turned off in Settings → Notifications.
• Time and date pickers follow your 12/24-hour and date format settings.
• Arabic and Persian show numbers, dates and counts with the right digits and punctuation.
• Tab labels fit at large text sizes, and error text is easier to read in dark mode.

Bug Fixes:
• Fixed a recipe’s grind size getting stuck after you tapped Next without changing it. It now follows the recipe again.
• Fixed the rating prompt appearing when you switched back to the app.
• Fixed notifications for your account still reaching a device after you signed out.
• Fixed the coffee level jumping when you pause an immersive brew.
