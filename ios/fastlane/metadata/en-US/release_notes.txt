What’s New:
• Spot a mistake on a roaster’s page? Tap ⋮ on the roaster profile and choose “Report an error”. Pick what’s wrong, add the correct value if you know it, and send it. We review every report.
• If you run a roastery, you can claim its page from the same menu, or from the “Is this your roastery?” link on the profile.

Improvements:
• The AI recipe check has been removed. The app now always checks the amounts in your recipe steps itself, and still asks you when an amount could mean either coffee or water.
• Roasters have one storefront icon throughout the app, and it stands in for a roaster’s logo until one is added.
