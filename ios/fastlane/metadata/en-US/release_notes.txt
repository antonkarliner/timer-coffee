What’s New:
• An immersive brewing screen: the whole screen fills with coffee as your brew goes on, and the brew ends with a last drop. Turn it on from the gear icon before you start a brew, or in Settings → Advanced / Beta Features. The classic timer is still there whenever you want it.
• Undo a delete: after deleting a brew, a bean or a recipe you get a few seconds to bring it back.
• Suggested a roaster we didn’t have yet? Once they’re on Timer.Coffee, you’ll see a thank-you with a link to their page.
• Review a scanned bag right on the bean form, instead of on a separate screen.

Improvements:
• A new ending for the classic timer: the ring closes and fills like a cup instead of shaking.
• On the last step, the button now reads “Finish” and shows up right away.
• Freshness reminders are rebuilt. They now nudge you about a bag you haven’t brewed yet as it reaches its peak, or one you’ve stopped brewing before its peak is over, and never months after roasting.
• A clearer recipe setup screen and an easier-to-read next-step preview while brewing.
• Numbers stay on the same line as their units in brew steps.

Bug Fixes:
• Fixed deleting a custom recipe also deleting every brew you had logged with it. Your brew diary now keeps those entries.
• Fixed deleting a bean removing its name from your past brews.
• Fixed freshness reminders almost never arriving.
• Fixed the “first brew” congratulations showing again on later brews.
