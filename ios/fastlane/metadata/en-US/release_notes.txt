What’s New:
• The First Steps card has a new last step: “Turn on brew updates”. Tap it to allow notifications right from the card.
• On the First Steps card, “Favorite a recipe” now opens the recipe you brewed most recently and highlights the heart, so you can save it in one tap.
• After two or more brews of the same recipe, the “Try another recipe” step now suggests a specific recipe for the same method. Tap it to open that recipe.
• After your first brew, you get one reminder the next morning instead of waiting until day five. It opens your recipe and doesn’t start a timer. You won’t get it if your daily morning reminder is already on.
• Haven’t brewed yet? Tap “Brew later” on any recipe to choose a time, and we’ll remind you with that recipe ready to go.

Improvements:
• Coffee freshness reminders are now on by default. If you turned them off before, they stay off. You can change this anytime in Settings → Notifications.
• If you’ve already opened the app in the morning, your daily morning reminder skips that day.
• When you write a recipe, the app more often recognizes which amounts in your steps are coffee and which are water.
• An empty Favorite Recipes list now shows how to add a recipe to it.

Bug Fixes:
• Fixed reminders not being set after you allowed notifications at the end of your first brew.
• Fixed the day 5 and day 10 brew reminders sometimes disappearing after you reopened the app.
• Fixed long bean and roaster names breaking in the middle of a word on the bean page.
• Fixed the “Best cup” label overlapping ratings on the bean journey chart.
