What’s New:
• Export your brew diary as Markdown — the whole diary, a single bean’s history, or one brew — so your notes are always yours to keep.
• Rate a brew right after you finish it: stars, taste, notes and tags in one quick sheet.
• Meet the Help Center: guides and answers about the app, available in every supported language.
• Export all your data from Settings → Privacy & Analytics: confirm by email and receive a downloadable archive.
• Jump straight to a bean’s journey from its brew history.

Improvements:
• Brew notes are now a proper part of each entry — more room to read them, and links you paste stay tappable.
• Grind size suggestions now draw on your brew history, not just your bean records.
• Improved translations across all supported languages.

Bug Fixes:
• Fixed water temperature and other numeric fields resetting or refusing to clear while you typed.
• Fixed suggestion dropdowns staying in place instead of following their field when the page scrolled.
• Fixed long field labels overflowing in the brew details view.
