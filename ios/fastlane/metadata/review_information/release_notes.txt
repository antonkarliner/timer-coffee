What’s New:
• Keep the photos from a coffee bag scan. Opt in while you scan and the camera shots are copied to your Photos at full quality — the app remembers your choice for next time.

Improvements:
• Scanning a bag is now one clear screen: review your photos, add a second one only if the bag needs it, see what the scan costs up front, and tap a single Analyze button.
• While a scan runs, the app says what it is doing — preparing your photos, reading the label — and lets you know when a slow connection is making it take longer.
• The Favorites screen now has a proper empty state, with a shortcut straight to the recipe list.

Bug Fixes:
• Fixed the scan screen looking frozen: the photo sheet stayed on top of the loading overlay and sat off-centre.
• Fixed the confirm checkmark sitting out of line when editing grind size on a recipe.
• Fixed low-contrast icons on the coffee beans empty and error screens in dark mode.
