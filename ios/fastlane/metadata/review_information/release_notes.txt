Improvements:
• Coffee bags often print a roast date with no year — “02/08” could mean 8 February or 2 August. When the label is unclear, or your photo doesn’t show a date at all, a scan now shows you exactly what was printed and asks you to confirm it, instead of quietly picking one reading. The date it read is already filled in, so confirming takes one tap and correcting it opens the usual date picker.
• The scan summary now flags an unclear roast date too, so you can spot it without scrolling down the form.

Bug Fixes:
• Fixed the brew date coming up empty when you add a brew to your diary by hand.
