This update adds new ways to discover, brew, and manage your coffee recipes.

New:
• Collections: browse curated recipe collections, like iced coffee recipes for hot days.
• Manual brew controls: move backward or forward during brewing when you need more control.
• Per-bag grind size: save a grind setting for each bag of beans.
• Two new brewing methods: Timemore B75 and Espro Bloom Brewer.
• Automatic translation for bean reviews.

Improved:
• More reliable unit conversion during recipe creation, with optional AI verification.
• Various UI and UX improvements throughout the app.

Fixed:
• Several small bugs.
